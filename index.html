02 • INTRODUCTION

Why First Response Matters

Cyber crime is one of the fastest-growing digital threats, affecting individuals, businesses and organizations.

  • Online Fraud: Financial and payment-related incidents can escalate quickly.
  • Phishing & Hacking: Victims may lose account access or sensitive information.
  • Evidence: Screenshots, transaction details, chats, emails and URLs may be important.
  • AI Assistance: The proposed system provides immediate, structured guidance.

04 • PROBLEM STATEMENT

The First-Response Gap

  • Victim Confusion: Panic and lack of structured immediate guidance.
  • Evidence Risk: Important digital information may not be preserved correctly.
  • Reporting Difficulty: Users may not know the appropriate official channel.
  • Need for Accessibility: Assistance should be simple and multilingual.

10 • DIGITAL EVIDENCE

Evidence Preservation

  • 📸 Screenshots — relevant transaction, message, profile or webpage.
  • 💳 Transaction Details — UTR, reference number, amount and time.
  • 📧 Emails — retain relevant original information where available.
  • 💬 Chat Records — relevant conversations and attachments.
  • 🔗 URLs / Links — suspicious or malicious links.
  • 📱 Device Information — relevant app/account/device details.
  • 🎙️ Audio / Voice — relevant recordings when lawfully obtained.

11 • AI + RAG

Why LLM + RAG?

🧠 LLM — Understand

Interprets natural-language incident descriptions and supports follow-up questions.

🔎 RAG — Retrieve

Retrieves relevant information from the project's knowledge base.

📚 Knowledge Base

Contains project-approved guidelines, procedures, FAQs and safety information.

🎯 Context

Combines incident information with retrieved context before response generation.

🌐 Multilingual

Supports the proposed English, Hindi and Marathi interaction model.

🛡 Responsible AI

Should communicate limitations and avoid unsupported legal or forensic conclusions.

14 • CONCLUSION

Conclusion

The proposed system combines AI, NLP, LLM, RAG and a cybercrime knowledge base to provide structured first-response assistance.

  • Identify possible incident category.
  • Assess immediate risk.
  • Ask relevant follow-up questions.
  • Guide evidence preservation.
  • Provide reporting and safety guidance.
  • Support multilingual interaction.
